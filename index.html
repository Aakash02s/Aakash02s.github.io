BUG-001

Cart total does not update after quantity change

REOPENED
SeverityHigh
PriorityHigh
TypeFunctional
EnvironmentChrome · Web
ReporterQA
StatusRetest Failed
STEPS TO REPRODUCE
  1. Add a product to cart.
  2. Open the cart.
  3. Change quantity from 1 to 2.
  4. Observe the total.

Previous total remains until the page is refreshed.
